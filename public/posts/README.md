# Meme artwork

Each meme slide names a file here, and every chapter's version of that meme is
built on it. Drop a file in with the right name and the post is finished for all
of them at once — nobody has to go and find a picture.

| File | What it wants |
| --- | --- |
| `street.jpg` | An ordinary road near campus. Cars, nothing dramatic. |
| `camera.jpg` | A plate reader, close, filling the frame. |
| `pole.jpg` | A camera on its pole, shot from below. |
| `meeting.jpg` | A council or board meeting — rows of seats, a lectern. |
| `breaking.jpg` | Kept generic; the reaction post is captioned per story. |

Square or landscape, 1200px on the short edge or more. They are drawn to cover a
1080×1080 square, so anything important belongs near the middle: the bottom
third sits under a scrim and the shouted line.

**Only use pictures the chapter is allowed to use.** Photographs somebody else
took are theirs, and a post built on a lifted image is the one that gets the
account reported rather than the cameras removed. A phone photo of the actual
pole outside your building is better than a stock image anyway — it is the one
nobody can argue with.

**Photographs of identifiable people are a separate decision.** Mocking a named
person's appearance is how a chapter stops being taken seriously, and it hands
the other side the only story they want. The jokes here land on the cameras.
